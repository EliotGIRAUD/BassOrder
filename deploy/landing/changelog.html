v0.1.8

Latest 25 August 2026

  • Site Legal notice: publisher email + full LWS host details; privacy and terms updated for contact and erasure rights.

v0.1.7

25 August 2026

New brand mark: a clean mint-on-black vector icon (membrane + dust cap), replaced across the app, installers, and landing.

First minutes in the app are cleaner: you start on your local library, search has its own page, and Spotify connects in one click. Launch opens on a BassOrder splash instead of a blank boot screen.

  • New Themed splash screen on launch.
  • Site Download nudge in the landing demo after a scan.
  • Site Search Console verification, sitemap MIME, brand mark in the README.
