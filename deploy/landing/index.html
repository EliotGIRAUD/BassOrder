BassOrder

From trash playlists to genre mastery.

BassOrder is a Windows desktop app (Mac coming soon) that turns messy Spotify likes / playlists and dumped local folders into Rock, Jazz, Hip-Hop, Electro, Pop, Metal… — you tweak the plan, then copy or move.

What it's for

From trash playlists to genre mastery

Clear goal: take a chaotic Spotify playlist (or a dumped folder of files) and sort it by music genres — not a social network, not a player. BassOrder detects / suggests the genre, you confirm, then the app organizes.

  1. 01

    Install & launch

    One space on your machine (handle + color). No Netflix-style multi-profiles.

  2. 02

    Connect Spotify (recommended)

    Import your likes / artists. BassOrder builds a genre dictionary for more accurate sorting later.

  3. 03

    Sort playlists & files

    Genre suggestions → corrections → copy or move into genre folders.

  4. 04

    Optional cloud

    Knowledge sync (mirror + pool). Your audio files stay on your PC.
